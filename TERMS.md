Last updated: October 14, 2026

## What PocketAlpaca is

PocketAlpaca is a personal, read-only viewer for an Alpaca brokerage
account. It displays information from your own Alpaca account on your own
iPhone. It is provided free of charge and is not listed on any app
marketplace.

## What it is not

- PocketAlpaca is not a broker, an investment adviser, or a trading

- Nothing shown in the app is investment advice or a recommendation.
- PocketAlpaca is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Alpaca
  Securities LLC or AlpacaDB, Inc. Alpaca is the broker; the app only
  reads data Alpaca makes available through its API.

You authorize the app through Alpaca's OAuth flow. You remain responsible
for your Alpaca account, its credentials, and the device the app is
installed on. Revoke the app's access at any time from your Alpaca

and may be delayed or incomplete. Your Alpaca account statements are the
authoritative record.
